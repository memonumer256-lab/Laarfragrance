ABOUT LAAR FRAGRANCE

Scent That Leaves A Memory.

Laar Fragrance is a Pakistani perfume brand created for people who believe that fragrance is more than just a scent — it is part of your identity.

Our Story

Laar Fragrance was created with a simple idea: to bring elegant, long-lasting fragrances to people in Pakistan at an accessible price.

Every fragrance is selected with attention to character, quality and everyday wearability. Our goal is to create scents that people remember.

Our Fragrances

From fresh and clean fragrances to deep oud, modern and warm scents, our collection is designed for different personalities and occasions.

Whether it is your everyday fragrance or something for a special evening, Laar Fragrance aims to make every spray memorable.

Our Promise

We focus on quality, presentation and customer experience. We want every Laar Fragrance order to feel like more than just a purchase.
