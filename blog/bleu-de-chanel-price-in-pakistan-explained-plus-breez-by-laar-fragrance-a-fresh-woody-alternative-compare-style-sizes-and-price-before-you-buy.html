What Is Bleu de Chanel Known For?

Bleu de Chanel is known for a clean, fresh opening with a citrus feel that settles into a smooth, woody and slightly spicy base. It is the kind of scent that people describe as classy, calm and confident. It does not smell loud or sweet, which is why it works for so many different people and occasions.

That balance is exactly why it has become a favourite for office wear, meetings, weddings and evenings out, and why so many people search for the Bleu de Chanel price in Pakistan every month.

How to Make Breez Last Longer
