How to Make Perfume Last Longer: 6 Simple Tips

Simple tips to make your perfume last longer: where to spray, how to store it, and why mini testers help you choose.

Want to make your perfume last longer? The right application and storage habits can help you enjoy your fragrance for longer. Whether you prefer a fresh scent like Breeze or a rich, woody scent like Ruby Oud, these six simple tips can help you get more out of your perfume.

1. Apply on moisturized skin

Perfume sticks better when skin is not dry. Apply lotion first and allow it to dry then apply your favorite perfume

2. Spray on Pulse Points

Warm areas like the wrists, neck, behind the ears help the scent open up. Spray 15-20 cm away

3. Don't rub your wrists

Rubbing the perfume kills the top notes of the fragrance. Let it dry

4. Apply after a shower

Warm and moist skin helps the perfume stick around longer. Spray within a few minutes of drying off

5. Store it properly

Heat and moisture can alter the perfume. Store your perfume in a cool and dry place away from direct sunlight

6. Try a mini tester first
