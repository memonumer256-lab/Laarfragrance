FRAGRANCE GUIDE

How To Choose Your Everyday Fragrance

Learn how to choose a fragrance that matches your style and everyday routine.

Choosing an everyday fragrance is about finding a scent that feels comfortable, natural and suitable for your lifestyle.

Fresh and clean fragrances are often easy to wear during the day, while warmer and deeper fragrances can work well for evenings.

The most important thing is to choose a fragrance that matches your personality and makes you feel confident.

PERFUME NOTES

Understanding Oud In Modern Fragrances

Discover why oud is one of the most distinctive and memorable fragrance notes.

Oud is one of the most recognizable ingredients in perfumery. It is known for its deep, woody and rich character.

Modern fragrances often combine oud with sweet, spicy, floral or fresh notes to create a more balanced scent.

Depending on the combination, oud can feel elegant, warm, powerful or mysterious.

FRAGRANCE TIPS

How To Make Your Perfume Last Longer

Simple tips that can help your fragrance stay noticeable throughout the day.

Applying perfume to moisturized skin can help the fragrance remain noticeable for longer.

Pulse points such as the wrists and neck are commonly used because they are warmer areas of the body.

Avoid rubbing your wrists together after applying perfume, as this can change the way the fragrance develops on the skin.
